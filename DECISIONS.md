## Open questions for the lawyer (Week 1–2 consult)

1. Is a paid, closed Telegram/Discord group publishing identical non-personalised spot signals "advice on crypto-assets" under MiCA Art. 3(1)(24) given ESMA Q&A 2882 (June 2026)? What features must be avoided (DM Q&A, tiers, "suitability" language)?
2. Does MAR Art. 2(1)(d) bring recommendations on offshore crypto perps into scope? We plan to publish none; confirm that acting as a copy-trading lead trader on an authorised exchange carries no "producer of recommendations" obligations for us, and what conflicts disclosure promoting our own lead account requires.
3. Any Bank of Lithuania / FNTT registration, notification or advertising rule for a company publishing free or paid crypto-asset information?
4. Consumer-law and privacy setup for a subscription: withdrawal-right waiver wording, imprint content (virtual office acceptable?), risk-warning wording, "Reklama" labelling, privacy policy and lawful basis for the email list.
5. Entity and payouts: MB vs UAB for two founders; affiliate/profit-share payouts in USDC received by the company (valuation, accounting, bank/EMI off-ramp, source-of-funds documentation); member payout structuring (PIT / Sodra); exact conditions and duration of the 0% start-up CIT rate.
6. Founder agreement: what a Lithuanian MB members' agreement can and cannot enforce (vesting, IP assignment, leaver terms).
