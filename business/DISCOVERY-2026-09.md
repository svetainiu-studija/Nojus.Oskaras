## The question

What, of what we actually built and proved, will real people pay for?
Not "would this be cool" — evidence of demand from strangers.

## Pre-registered GO criteria (ALL required by 2026-09-11)

1. **≥ 15 structured conversations** with people in the target group
   (retail traders who backtest or buy signals; algo-curious manual
   traders), logged one line each in this file's OUTCOME section.
2. **≥ 5 of them describe the problem unprompted** — losing money to
   strategies/signals that "backtested well", not knowing what to trust
   — before any pitch is made.
3. **≥ 3 concrete commitment signals** at a named price (€19–49/month
   tool or €99+ one-off strategy audit): waitlist signup with a real
   email, a prepay offer, or a written "I'd buy this when it exists".
   Kind words are not commitment.
4. **≥ 1 repeatable ~€0 channel** where these people were actually
   reached (a specific subreddit, Discord/Telegram group, forum — with
   evidence the posts/DMs got responses, not bans).

Fail any → **NO-GO** → PIVOT-DISCUSSION option b (archive with a
post-mortem), decided at that point, not before. Pass all → CHARTER
v2.0 + a build plan for exactly what the conversations validated.

## The three candidate offers (test all three, let evidence rank them)

- **O1 — Strategy audit (service, €99–299 one-off):» You think your
  strategy works. We'll prove it — or show you why it doesn't, before
  the market does.« The audit battery this repo just ran on itself
  (costs, look-ahead, baselines, concentration, multiplicity), applied
  to a customer's rules/backtest. Highest price, zero build needed —
  the engine exists.
- **O2 — Honest backtesting tool (product, €19–49/mo):» Pre-registered
  backtests: write the rule down, then run it — with real costs and a
  random baseline built in.« Needs real build; test demand only.
- **O3 — Education/content (audience first, revenue later):» We spent a
  week trying to prove we had an edge. We didn't. Here's the machine
  that stopped us losing our savings.« The negative result as the hook;
  monetization later (courses/tools) — slowest, cheapest.

Positioning rule (legal, unchanged): no signals, no advice, no
performance promises. We sell verification and education — outside the
MiCA advice perimeter by construction (see legal/ research).

## Interview guide (Mom-Test style — past behaviour, never pitching first)

1. When did you last trade a strategy or signal you didn't fully trust?
   What happened?
2. How did you decide it was trustworthy? What did you check?
3. Have you ever paid for signals, courses, or tools? Which? Worth it?
4. What's the most money a "proven" backtest or signal has cost you?
5. If you could verify one thing about a strategy before risking money,
   what would it be?
6. (Only after all the above) show O1/O2 one-liners: "Would you use
   this? What would you pay?" — then, if positive: "Can I put you on
   the waitlist / would you prepay at €X?" (the commitment test).
