# 4. Why EXP-007 Does Not Clear the Standard

The conclusion does not rest on one metric; the candidate fails
simultaneously across several dimensions.

- **Statistical evidence:** p remains above the decision threshold under
  both baseline constructions.
- **Multiple testing:** Holm correction does not rescue the result.
- **Concentration:** SOL produces **148% of total profit**.
- **Leave-one-out:** removing the single dominant trade converts the
  result from profitable to materially negative.
- **Temporal robustness:** fails **3 of 7** chronological folds.
- **Cross-sectional robustness:** excluding SOL leaves **−0.159 R**.
- **Trade count:** only 48 trades — too small for the stability the
  project's own rules require.
- **Stress** *(clarified in verification)*: EXP-007 itself **passed**
  the 2× cost stress (PF 1.50 ≥ 1.1). The stress failure belongs to its
  4h sibling EXP-006 (stress PF 0.96, 52.9% drawdown): the strategy
  *family's* resilience failed, not this specific run's cost
  robustness. EXP-007's failures are the structural ones above.

Taken together, the evidence is consistent with **a weak or accidental
historical result**, not a statistically defensible edge.

# 8. Bugs Found and Fixed (permanent record, all regression-tested)

**Bug 1 — Monte Carlo baseline.** Used random-entry / same-HOLDING-
PERIOD rather than the declared random-entry / same-EXIT. This
construction could favour the strategy. Replaced with the compliant
same-exit baseline.

**Bug 2 — "+1R reached" semantics.** Measured on candle closes while
the declared 3R partial filled intrabar — inconsistent semantics.
Corrected to intrabar behaviour.

**Bug 3 — Spot cash floor.** The simulator could temporarily model
exposure exceeding account equity — incompatible with an unleveraged
spot account. Now enforced, including the small-order bound from
`costs.yaml`. The violation was **never active in EXP-007** (verified
by rerun counters).

# 16. Does the Evidence Justify Reopening Research?

# NO.

There has been no new information source, no discovered error that
changes the verdict, no new mechanism demonstrated testable within the
charter, and no result invalidating the stopping rule. A longer or more
sophisticated prompt is not new information. A new strategy idea is not
automatically new information. A desire for a better outcome is not new
information. Reopening solely because the answer is disappointing would
recreate the exact research behaviour the founders established controls
to prevent.

# 17. The Reopening Rule (a future D-025)

Research may reopen in the future — only through a new joint founder
decision explicitly stating: **what** reopens (strategy / information
domain / data source); **why** D-024 is overridden; **what is genuinely
new**; the **experiment cap**; the **stopping rules** (defined before
results are known); and **what cannot change retroactively** (the
validation standard, holdout protection, research governance).
