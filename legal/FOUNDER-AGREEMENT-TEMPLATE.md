# Founder Agreement — TEMPLATE (v0.1, 2026-08-27)

**Status: template for the founders to fill in and for the lawyer to review (lawyer question 6). Not legal advice. Not signed.**
Fill every `[ ]`, delete what doesn't apply, have it reviewed, then both sign two paper copies (or qualified e-signatures). The operative version should be prepared in Lithuanian; this English template fixes the *content* you two need to agree on.

Design constraints already baked in:
- Under the MB law, **work or services cannot be a member's contribution** — only money/assets. Effort is recognised through the profit-split rationale, not as an įnašas.
- Transferring MB member rights to new members within the **first three years** after registration voids the 0% CIT relief (PMĮ 5 str., verified 2026-08-27). Therefore vesting below is **contractual and economic** (buy-out formulas), never staged transfers of membership.
- IP assignment must be **specific and documented** — a vague "everything belongs to the company" is not reliably enforceable.

## 1. Parties and purpose

This agreement is between **Oskaras [full name]** and **Nojus [full name]** (together, "the Founders"), who are building a pseudonymous crypto trading-intelligence business ("the Venture") as described in the CHARTER (v1.1) of the repository `svetainiu-studija/Nojus.Oskaras`, which both Founders have read and adopted.

Until a mažoji bendrija ("the MB") is registered (planned before the first revenue payout, D-006), the Venture is an unincorporated collaboration governed by this agreement. On registration, both Founders become the MB's only members and this agreement continues as a members' agreement to the extent the MB law allows.

## 2. Ownership and profit split

- Member rights and profit split: **Oskaras [50]% / Nojus [50]%**.
- Rationale (recorded so future-us remembers): Oskaras builds and operates all systems; Nojus owns brand, distribution, operations and external programmes; both contribute strategy hypotheses and weekly work. The split reflects [equal / other: ______] expected contribution over the first 24 months.
- Monetary contributions: each Founder contributes **€[  ]** toward the budget (D-016 ceiling €5,000 total). Contributions are logged in the expense sheet. If contributions become unequal by more than €[500], the Founders either rebalance in cash or adjust the profit split by written addendum.

## 3. Vesting (contractual buy-out — no membership transfers in years 1–3)

- Each Founder's economic interest "vests" over **[24] months** from [date], in [monthly] increments.
- If a Founder leaves before full vesting, they keep their MB membership only if the MB's 0% CIT relief would be endangered by a transfer; otherwise membership handling follows §6. In all cases the **economic settlement** is:
  - **Good leaver** (health, family emergency, mutual agreement): entitled to (a) return of unspent monetary contribution, plus (b) [vested %] of the Venture's net profit for the following **[12] months**, paid [quarterly], and nothing after.
  - **Bad leaver** (walks away, breach of this agreement, inactivity per §7): entitled to return of unspent monetary contribution only.
- After month [24], both Founders are fully vested and §6 governs any exit.

## 4. Intellectual property

All of the following, whether created before or after MB registration, is Venture property and will be assigned to the MB in writing upon registration (until then, each Founder holds their creations for the Venture's benefit and may not license or sell them elsewhere):

source code (engine, backtester, poster, reporting); trading strategies, their parameters, configs and hashes; strategy documentation and research (hypotheses, experiments, datasets and dataset versions); the brand name, logo, visual identity and any trademarks; domains; the website and its content; Telegram/Discord channels and bots; social-media accounts and handles; GitHub organisation and repositories; the email/member list; performance records and the signal log; this repository.

Each Founder will sign the specific copyright-assignment document the lawyer prepares (autorių turtinių teisių perdavimas) listing these items.

## 5. Accounts, credentials, security

- Root of control is a **Venture-owned email** on the Venture's domain — never a personal email — for: domain registrar, GitHub org, Telegram/Discord admin, social accounts, exchange accounts, payment providers, hosting.
- All credentials live in the shared vault **[vault name]**; both Founders hold access; 2FA on everything; recovery codes stored [where]. No account may have a single Founder as its only possible recoverer.
- Exchange and payment accounts are opened in [the MB's name once registered / the agreed Founder's name until then, held for the Venture].

## 6. Exit, deadlock, death

- A Founder may exit with **[60] days'** written notice. The remaining Founder may buy the leaver's interest at: [contributed capital not yet returned] + [ [50]% of the trailing-12-month net profit × their % ]. Payable over [12] months. (Formula deliberately simple — lawyer to sanity-check.)
- **Deadlock** on a material decision (a D-number both refuse to sign): 30-day cooling period, then mediation [LT mediators' service]; if still deadlocked after [90] days, the Venture's automated systems are put in safe pause and either Founder may trigger the buy-out clause at the §6 price with the *other* Founder choosing buyer/seller side ("shotgun" — lawyer to confirm enforceability for an MB).
- On a Founder's death or incapacity, their economic interest passes to their heirs but **operational control does not**; the surviving Founder runs the Venture and pays the heirs per §6's formula.

## 7. Commitment and inactivity

- Each Founder commits **10–20 h/week** (CHARTER §2), except agreed exam windows (Dec–Jan, May–Jun rota).
- If a Founder averages under **[8] h/week for [6] consecutive non-exam weeks** without agreement, the other may serve a written warning; a further [4] such weeks make them a **bad leaver** under §3 at the other's option.

## 8. Key-person duties

- Oskaras maintains the runbook so that Nojus can restart, pause, or flatten the engine alone, and demonstrates this [quarterly].
- Nojus maintains a documented handover of brand accounts, programme contacts and payment-rail admin so Oskaras can operate them alone.

## 9. Money discipline

- Budget ceiling per D-016: **€5,000** total risk capital; no spend above **€[100]** without both Founders' OK (logged).
- No Venture income is received into personal accounts once the MB exists (D-006, T15). Member payouts follow the accountant's model (note: VSD base on withdrawals is 90% from 2026-07-01).

## 10. Confidentiality and non-compete

- Strategy parameters, code and the member list are confidential indefinitely, including after exit.
- During the Venture and for **[12] months** after leaving, a Founder will not run or join a directly competing crypto-signal or copy-trading service targeting the same audience, and will not solicit the Venture's members. (Lawyer to confirm enforceable scope under LT law.)

## 11. General

- Material changes to the Venture are decided per the DECISIONS.md process; this agreement changes only in writing signed by both.
- Governed by Lithuanian law; disputes: good-faith negotiation → mediation → Lithuanian courts, [Vilnius].
