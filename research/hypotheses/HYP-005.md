Name: Momentum leaders with ATR stops at 4h timescale (bar-parity windows)

  4h bars (canonical derived series). Rules of HYP-004 with windows in BARS:
  - Leader: 60-bar (10-day) return minus BTC's, top 5 of the universe.

  RS exit on leaving the top half; 15-bar (~2.5-day) time stop if never +1R;
  regime exit on BTC < 50-bar SMA; 1% risk, D-015 caps.

  Point-in-time top-30 OKX spot, 4h derived bars.

  The same momentum mechanism operates at shorter horizons in crypto's
  24/7 retail-driven tape; 6x more evaluation points and faster cycles
  give the trade count a realistic path to the G0 floor. Costs bite
