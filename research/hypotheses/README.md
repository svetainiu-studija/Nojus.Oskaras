# Strategy hypotheses (task 0.8)

One file per hypothesis: `HYP-001.md`, `HYP-002.md`, `HYP-003.md` — blank templates ready to fill. `EXAMPLE.md` shows the level of detail needed; it is **an illustration, not one of your hypotheses**.

Rules (from PLAN-90-DAYS, protocol step 1):

1. **Hypothesis first.** Write the economic reason the edge exists — who is on the other side of your trade and why they lose — *before* touching any data. No reason → no sprint.
2. **Rules a computer can check.** "I buy when it looks strong" is not a rule. "Close above the 20-day high" is. If you can't write the rule, describe the situations precisely and we'll formalise together in the build session.
3. **Be honest in the last field.** Your real discretionary experience — including the worst period — decides which hypothesis we test first.
4. Don't overthink formatting. Rough answers in every field beat perfect answers in two.

Bring the filled files to the next build session — they are the input for the private strategy repo and the backtester (task 0.9).
